## Target roles

Senior technical leadership without direct reports. The target is the
IC-track leadership seat: Principal or Distinguished Engineer, senior
architect, platform-owning technical lead — authority over architecture
and technical direction, no people management. Direct-report management
is not wanted; roles whose core is managing managers are not a fit.

The clearest picture of the target is the Thoughtworks-style architect:
someone who builds proof-of-concept AI frameworks, platforms, and
applications, presents the working solution to the customer, and hands
off the design for the customer's team to productionize. Forward
Deployed Engineer/Architect seats (LangChain's Deployed Architect mold,
OpenAI's Forward Deployed Engineering, Principal Consultant at an
AI-forward consultancy) are exactly this shape. The fit test: authors
architecture, builds the PoC hands-on, presents to senior stakeholders,
no sales quota, no standing org to manage.

In platform engineering terms, the same test applies: the seat that
owns infrastructure, deployment, observability, and developer experience
through technical authority rather than org authority — provided it is
hands-on and IC-track, not a manager of the people who are.

Twenty years of technical leadership experience is context for the
judgment, not the job being sought. Player-coach and hands-on-builder framing both
fit; a pure people-management track does not.
